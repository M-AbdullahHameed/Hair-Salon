# Discovery Questionnaire — Hair Salon Website

Send this to the salon owner before any design work. Answers drive the site map, mood board and copy.

## 1. Business basics
1. Salon name, address, opening hours, phone, booking email.
2. How long have you been open? Any story behind the name or the space?
3. Who are the stylists? (names, specialties, years of experience, photos)
4. Do you sell retail products? Which brands?

## 2. Services & pricing
5. Full service list with prices (or "from" prices) and durations.
6. Which 3 services make you the most money? Which do you want to grow?
7. Do prices vary by stylist level (junior / senior / creative director)?
8. Do you offer consultations, bridal, men's grooming, extensions, colour correction?

## 3. Ideal client
9. Describe your ideal client: age, lifestyle, what they spend per visit, how often they come.
10. Why do they choose you over the salon down the street?
11. What worries a new client before their first visit? (price, result, stylist fit)
12. What do clients compliment most after a visit?

## 4. How clients find & book you today
13. Where do new clients come from? (Instagram, TikTok, Google Maps, referrals, walk-ins, ads)
14. Current booking method: phone, DM, Fresha / Booksy / Vagaro / Square, other?
15. Should the website take bookings directly, or hand off to your booking system?
16. Any deposits, cancellation or late policy that must be shown?

## 5. Trust & proof
17. Google / Fresha rating and number of reviews. Can we feature them?
18. Before/after photos we have permission to use?
19. Awards, press, certifications, brand partnerships (e.g. Olaplex, Kérastase)?
20. Any numbers worth bragging about? (clients served, years, 5★ reviews)

## 6. Brand & look
21. Do you have a logo and brand guidelines (fonts, colours)? Please attach.
22. The shop interior is **black and white marble, high-end**. Should the site mirror the space exactly, or elevate it?
23. Is the feel **premium** (polished, modern, accessible — think Aesop, Apple) or **luxury** (exclusive, rare, ornate — think Chanel, Rolex)?
24. Pick 3–5 words for how a client should feel landing on the site (e.g. calm, confident, pampered, editorial, bold).
25. Share 3 websites (any industry) whose look you love, and why.
26. Share 2–3 salons or beauty businesses you see as successful, and what you admire.
27. Anything you never want the site to look or feel like?

## 7. Goals
28. The #1 action a visitor should take (book, call, DM, buy product)?
29. Do you need to update content yourself (prices, team, gallery)?
30. Launch deadline and any upcoming events or promotions?
